Research

My work connects learning theory, reinforcement learning, statistical inference, and algorithmic game theory. I am particularly interested in agents that learn from partial observations, restricted feedback, and strategic interaction.

01

Information & representation

Minimal predictive states, partial observability, and the distinction between information needed for prediction and information needed for decisions.

02

Learning & decision-making

Exploration, realizability, sample complexity, oracle complexity, and Bellman structure in reinforcement learning.

03

Feedback & interaction

Preference identifiability, learning in games, and the limits imposed by the feedback available to a learner.
